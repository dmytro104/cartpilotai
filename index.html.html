FAQ

Do you replace Klaviyo?

No. CartPilot AI runs inside Klaviyo. We install flows and reporting; no rip‑and‑replace.

How do you prove incremental lift?

We maintain a 10% control group (holdout) and attribute lift to the AI flows only.

Will this hurt deliverability?

No. We use warmed domains, safe volume caps, and compliant templates with one‑click opt‑out.

What access do you need?

Shopify collaborator access and Klaviyo admin. No PII is stored outside these platforms.

Can we start with a pilot?

Yes. As a founding client, you effectively get a pilot with full implementation at no service fee.

Does this work with SMS consent?

Email works for all; SMS flows respect consent and quiet hours (TCPA/CAN‑SPAM).
